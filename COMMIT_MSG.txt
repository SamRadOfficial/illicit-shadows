Fine print
- "Donations are not tax deductible" is now 10px mono at 0.7 opacity.

  `.support-fine` class, so the rule had been silently losing and the line was
  rendering at body size, 16.5px. The selector now matches that specificity.
  Contrast measured 14.7:1, so it is quiet without being unreadable.
