Museum prototype: stop the viewer scrolling the site

The bug: /museum/enter put the 3D viewer inside a normal page, with a hero above
it and a band and the footer below. In first-person mode the viewer did not
capture the wheel, the arrow keys, space or page keys, so they fell through and
scrolled the website. The view shifted under the camera and navigation ended up
pointing at the ceiling.

Viewer (public/museum-viewer.html)
- Wheel, scroll keys (Space, arrows, PageUp/Down, Home, End) and touch drags now
  have their default action cancelled in every mode, not only the overview.
  Movement keys still reach the game: this cancels the scroll, it does not stop
  the event.
- html and body are overflow:hidden with overscroll-behavior:none.
- The viewer takes keyboard focus on load and on any click.

Page (app/museum/enter, components/ImmersiveLock.jsx)
- The viewer fills the screen below the nav, fixed, with nothing above or below
  it to scroll to. ImmersiveLock locks page scrolling while mounted and restores
  it when the visitor leaves.
- The hero and the back-to-museum band are gone; the viewer has its own title
  bar, and the nav stays visible for leaving.

Verified: host page scrollY stays 0 after twelve wheel ticks and eight keys on
desktop, and after three long touch drags on a phone; scrolling is restored on
/museum after leaving. The 3D scene itself could not be checked here: Three.js
loads from a CDN this sandbox blocks, identically before and after the change.
Check on the live site.

Deploy: this changes public/museum-viewer.html, so the ASSETS zip for public/ is
needed as well as the source zip.
