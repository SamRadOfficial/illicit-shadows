Metadata audit: canonicals everywhere, real descriptions on every page

Audited all 30 public pages for title, description, canonical, og and twitter
tags. Fixed:

- **20 pages had no canonical.** Every investigation, dispatch, newsroom post,
  and the preview, enter and sources pages now emit one. Without them the
  preview host and the live domain look like duplicate copies of each other.
- **Descriptions were 28 to 56 characters** on the film and dispatch pages,
  which is far below what a search result shows. Investigations now lead with
  the subtitle and continue into the synopsis; dispatches name the film, the
  position in the sequence and the subject. All clamped to 155 on a word
  boundary.
- **Newsroom post titles ran to 160 characters.** The meta title is now trimmed
  to 40 plus the site suffix; the full headline still appears on the page.
- Removed two stale second descriptions, on /donate and /shop, that were
  overriding the good ones written below them, and gave /books/preview its own
  description instead of the site boilerplate.
- New helpers in lib/og.js: canonical(path) and clamp(text, max).

Two left deliberately: the Fentanyl's Dirty Profits dispatch title is 66
characters, six over what Google displays, and /specimen has no canonical
because it is the internal type specimen and is disallowed in robots.

Nothing in the built metadata says "docuseries". The live domain still serves
Squarespace, which is where that wording is coming from.

Verified: build 34 pages, 194 references present. Zero duplicate titles or
descriptions across the site.
