Film submenu with covers

  upcoming slate, and a link to the index. Built from films.json, released
  first, so a new film appears without touching the nav.
- Hover opens it on a pointer device; the chevron button opens it on touch and
  from the keyboard and pins it open, so hover cannot close what the button
  opened. Escape and an outside click close it; navigating closes it. Film
  itself stays a link to /film.
- Below 820px it is an indented sub-list inside the hamburger panel.
- Items stack the cover above the label; side by side the titles wrapped in the
  narrow columns.
