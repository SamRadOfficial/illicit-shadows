Share cards, noindex on the preview host, redirect map, viewer copy

OG images
- scripts/og-images.py generates twelve 1200x630 cards from the site's own art:
  page title in Anton over a darkened crop, wordmark bottom left. The two film
  covers are used as-is, since they already carry their title and branding.
- lib/og.js wires a card into a page's metadata in one line. Every route now
  declares its own card and its own twitter:summary_large_image; newsroom posts
  use the post's image where it has one. Verified: no page points at a card
  that does not exist on disk.

Robots
- The preview host gets X-Robots-Tag: noindex, nofollow through a host-matched
  header rule in vercel.json. robots.txt is a single static file and cannot vary
  by host, and a full duplicate of the site competing with the real domain is
  worse than no preview. The site itself stays open to crawlers.

Redirects
- 31 rules, since Squarespace is being retired entirely rather than kept as a
  redirect host. Covers both old news URLs by name, /news and /news/:slug, and
  the paths most likely to be linked from elsewhere: /shadowverse, /episodes,
  /watch, /mis, /helix, /store, /team, /subscribe and others.
- Note: these run on Vercel, not in the static export, so a local preview will
  404 them. Nothing load-bearing depends on them.

Museum viewer
- Replaced SEASON 2 / GOLDEN HANDCUFFS / JANUARY 2027 with Illicit Gold and
  2026-2027, and removed the remaining Shadowverse references. This is canvas
  text inside the viewer, invisible to a site-wide grep.

Verified: build 34 pages, 184 references present. vercel.json parses; 31 of 32
built pages carry a card, the exception being the standalone viewer.
