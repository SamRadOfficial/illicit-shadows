About the EpisodeSYNOPSIS

Episode 1 explores the economic, societal, and human costs of the deadly illicit fentanyl pipelines — and the shadowy networks expanding and fueling greater illicit economies across North America, including at our borders and using our own infrastructure against us.

From the streets of San Francisco to the cartel fentanyl labs in Mexico and Canada; from the Chinese chemical companies to Canadian ports, we follow the chemical trail back through illicit supply chains — inter-modal transportation nodes of maritime shipping, highways, courier services, banking, real estate, e-commerce, and social media apps.

We bring greater insight to policy makers, law enforcement, and victimized communities on today's illegal fentanyl trade — as it finances other criminal activities and greedy profits are laundered in financial safe havens that converge across Mexico and the United States.
